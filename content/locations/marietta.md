## Love County Courthouse Information

### Love County Courthouse
**Address:** 405 W Main St, Marietta, OK 73448  
**Phone:** (580) 276-3053 (Court Clerk)  
**Hours:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM  

The Love County Courthouse, built in 1910, was the first courthouse erected after Oklahoma statehood. District court filings for Love County are handled here.

### Practical Notes
- Parking is available around the downtown square near Main Street
- Clerk lines are busiest at opening and near the 4:30 PM filing cutoff
- Casino and interstate traffic around Thackerville can add drive time on I-35
